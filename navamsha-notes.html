Chapter one

The chart you cannot touch

Navamsha is your subtle sharira, your subtle existence, your mantra sharira. It also carries very high importance whenever marriage is being analysed. Both are true at once, and which one is speaking depends on the question you brought.

By now you should be able to look at the degrees of a graha and estimate for yourself which navamsha it falls in, whether it is Vargottama, whether it sits in Pushkaramsha. That much is assumed. What follows goes further than a first semester normally goes, and he said so himself: "I have never taught this in first semester. I know it is too little, too early, but still I wanted to share."

One distinction has to be set down before anything else, because the whole of the next chapter rests on it. The Lagna of the rashi chart is one thing. The Lagna of the Navamsha is your dharma, and it works at the subtle level.हिHindi class Hold that, and the 12th house from it stops being a mystery.

Because the chart is subtle, it is also delicate. Two questions from the room made that concrete.

?

Two babies born in the same hospital, in rooms side by side. Same planets, same everything. Will they have the same life?

It cannot happen in the same moment. If at all, there has to be a difference of 24 seconds, one vighati. Only one human soul can come in each vighati. An animal soul can, but only one human soul. "A universal law which nobody knows."

And the vighati moves with space: every 27 to 28 kilometres, the local time shifts by that same 24 seconds. So two babies can be born in the same second if they are that far apart. Watch a population clock and five numbers appear at once; they are in different parts of the world.

Chapter two

One house, read from three places

Last week covered the 10th, the 4th, the 7th, the 8th and the trine of 1, 5, 9. One house in Navamsha had still not been given its due, and it is the one nobody talks about.

From D1 Lagna

The gross letting go

What one gives up and renounces, what you step back from, where the money goes. Expenditure, withdrawal, vairagya in the material world.

From D9 Lagna

The end of your dharma

Sleep, bed, private life, the intimate dimension. Thoughts coming to a stop and the mind emptying, so meditation deepens. And, because the D9 Lagna is dharma, the expenditure of dharma itself.

From Karakamsha

The real dissolution

Moksha, emancipation, release of the soul. The true ahamkara antakara: the dissolution of the self happens here, and nowhere else.

Why the D9 twelfth cuts both ways

Take the good side first. This house empties the mind. Your thoughts stop, and that is what takes you deep in meditation and gives you good sleep, when the 12th of Navamsha is sound.हिHindi class

Now the cost. The Navamsha Lagna is your dharma, so the 12th from it is the expenditure of dharma, and dharma rests on ahamkara. Your commitment, your ethics, the processes you keep, the moral values you hold, all of them stand on some form of "I am a good man, this is right, this is how it should be." There is dur-ahamkara and there is sad-ahamkara, and they are not the same thing: the ego that keeps you on dharma is the good kind. Dissolve it in the subtle and you may also drift off dharma.हिHindi class

Which is why this house carries what it carries in marriage. Count it from the partner: the 7th of Navamsha is your partner, so your 12th is the 6th from them, the house of enmity towards your own spouse. Combinations here can cause promiscuity, and he named the shape of them without opening the subject: Venus going into the D9 twelfth, Mars arriving with it; Mars in a Venus rashi, Venus alongside; Venus in a Mars rashi; Venus there with Saturn's drishti falling on it.हिHindi class The detail waits for the marriage chapter, which is being extended next semester precisely for this.

So when you put the 12th house on the Navamsha, put it there in the context of dharma and of marriage. It does not simply mean expense.

When I teach this one, nobody talks about the 12th house of Navamsha anyway.

Chapter three

Karakamsha, where the whole chart looks

There is one point in Navamsha that is the most crucial of all. The moment it is defined, your entire focus goes there: the rashi holding your Chara Atma Karaka in D9. Karaka plus Amsha gives Karakamsha. This is your spiritual seed, and it shows where your ignorance has settled at the subtle level.

Chara Atma Karaka → its rashi in D9 → Karakamsha → the environment working on your Atma

There is a whole Karakamsha Kundali as well, cast by treating the Karakamsha as the Lagna and reading every house from there. That belongs to the spiritual Jyotish chapter; this is the indication only.हिHindi class

The bhava it falls in matters. But the rashi matters more, because a rashi is an environment, and an environment constantly changes whoever sits inside it.

He made it physical. Suppose your nature is Sun-prominent: at home, in the office, you are the boss, a king. Now seat that king among artists, among creative people, and make him live his whole life there. Slowly the king in you finishes and an artist is born.हिHindi class That is what the rashi is doing to your Atma Karaka. Such people show the qualities of their Atma Karaka at the start, and gradually take on the qualities of the rashi it is sitting in. The environment is changing the ignorance itself.

Which gives the striking consequence: whoever you are in this lifetime, what you will be in the next is read from the rashi of the Atma Karaka. There is a view that the lord of that navamsha rashi becomes your Atma Karaka, your ignorance-carrier, in the next birth. If it sits in a Venus amsha, the Atma aligns with Bhuvar Loka: material pleasure, relationship, beauty, glamour, music, art. In a Sun amsha, with the Manipura, probably Swarga Loka.

He was careful here. There are texts that claim to show which loka a soul came from and which it is going to. There is no evidence; nobody comes back to confirm it. Very specific grahas are assigned to each loka in different scriptures, and looking at real lives he could not relate to them, so the whole system was left out of this curriculum. Someone good, kind and spiritual would show as heading to Narka Loka. We stay with the rashi placement only, because that much is logical.

And ignorance is what we are talking about all along. Agyan hi ahamkara hai. Ignorance is ego. So the end of that ego is read from the 12th from Karakamsha, which is the next chapter.

The rashi he was asked about, and the chart he opened to prove itहिHindi class

Meena amsha, and why satsang is what holds people

Parashara: one whose Atma Karaka sits in Meena amsha is a very mature soul. Spiritual, detached, knowledgeable, pious, near moksha. But look at who owns Meena and it opens up: not Jupiter alone, Jupiter and Ketu Maharaj together, param gyan and vairagya. Highest knowledge with total detachment. That combination makes a saint, and it turns up only in the chart of someone who arrived carrying that level of maturity.

Then the detail almost nobody states: the last navamsha of every water sign is Meena amsha. So if the Atma Karaka is already in a water rashi and then lands in Meena amsha, water is meeting water, and the path is bhakti. Bhajan, kirtan, sankirtan. That is what carries such a soul to mukti.

Gurudev's own chart

He asked the class a question first. Apart from Gurudev himself, what is the one thing in the organisation that binds people to it most? People joined for many reasons, but the majority join through satsang, because satsangs like that do not happen anywhere else.

Then the reason. Gurudev's Atma Karaka is Jupiter, exalted, at 29 degrees of Cancer, which puts it in Meena amsha. A water sign Atma Karaka landing in the Meena amsha of that water sign. So a great Guru shakti has come to the earth with an exalted Atma Karaka, and that shakti is turning into a Guru shakti that is also completely detached, because Meena is moving towards total maturity through Ketu Maharaj.

Two things only: the dignity of the Atma Karaka, and the rashi it has gone into. From that you can see what kind of soul has come and which way it is changing.

He then pulled it straight back. Meena amsha will happen to every twelfth person, and every twelfth person is not a saint. Do not take this as a dictum. It is one sutra. When the spirituality module is taught in full he will give forty more like it, and then you check how many are fulfilled and how many are not, and only from that do you say how much inclination towards spirituality there is.

The story he told

Snakes and ladders was a spiritual instrument first

The game we know is the frivolous version of Moksha Sopan. The original had 72 boxes, eight and nine, and each row carried the nine qualities of one chakra, from Muladhara upward, with the eighth level above everything.

You play it with something personal that has held your energy a long time, a ring, an earring, something that can represent you. After five or six rounds of dice it shows you which chakra your energy is actually stuck at. He taught it in the ashram, where it became very popular. In Nepal he built it human size on a 25 by 25 foot platform, and people stood inside the board and played, and had amazing experiences of where their lives were moving and where they were stuck. He shared it with Gurudev about fifteen years ago, and Bharatma kept the board and the book.

The lesson from the board. In the Agnya chakra there is a ladder of bhakti that leads straight to moksha, and right beside it a snake of arrogance, because bhakti brings a little egoistic arrogance too. So even if your chart shows the Atma has reached the pinnacle in Meena amsha, that is not a guarantee. It means you have reached there. Malefics and malefic aspects are the snakes that pull you back, and you begin again. Equally, if you are not there, a ladder may still carry you up: an exchange. And that exchange can be aspected by a node, a snake sitting there, biting you back down.

There is nothing certain till you are enlightened. The only certainty is the presence of a Guru who will eventually lead you. Not necessarily this lifetime.

Chapter four

What can actually dissolve it

The Atma Karaka represents your agyan, your ignorance. The rashi it sits in changes that ignorance but never dissolves it. The only thing with the ability to dissolve it is the 12th from Karakamsha. And what sits there decides which part of the ignorance goes, and which part stays.

He worked the elements out loud. A graha of water in that house will dissolve the salt inside you completely, and the sugar. It will not touch the plastic in you, or the metal. Air cannot dissolve; it can shake things a little. Earth will get there eventually, in two hundred, three hundred, five hundred years. What burns everything immediately is fire.हिHindi class That is why fire is called Pavaka, the purifier: the one that purifies by dissolving impurities, and why tapah is done by sustaining and igniting five kinds of fire within us.

So a fiery graha in the 12th from Karakamsha is what makes you do tapasya and dissolves your karmas. In a watery sign the fire is not lit properly. In an airy sign there is too much air and the fire is unsteady. A fiery graha in a fiery sign works fantastic.

Ketu's fire Moksha Karaka

Put any graha in that house and none of them dissolves the ego completely. Only Ketu Maharaj converts a thing into smoke and ash, into vibhuti. And ash is the last part: nothing remains that could be burned any further, nothing goes beyond it. That is the power sitting in the 12th from Karakamsha.

Even Ketu is conditioned by the rashi. In a water sign he will let a few things go, saying leave it. In air he will not pay enough attention, and whatever remains, remains. In earth he will do it in perfection and take a very long time about it. In a fire sign, it is ash.हिHindi class

And the burning needs support so that nothing obstructs it, which is where the drishti comes in. Jupiter looking at that Ketu is the eye of knowledge. If not Jupiter, then Venus, and the thing gets burned through bhakti. Or the Moon, and it is burned by love, by karuna. Any benefic drishti on that Ketu, and the scriptures say such a person is liberated in this life.हिHindi class

When Ketu transits your Moon you feel everything shattering, your world shattered. Unless you have already given up this world from your heart. Say "this is not my world" and Ketu Maharaj will not touch it. Say "mine, mine, mine" and everything gets burned.

An exalted graha in that house shows the person going into that kind of realm at a higher level. Conjunction or exchange with Ketu and you turn into a Devanshu. Atma Karaka exalted shows you are not an ordinary soul: great sankalpa shakti, great willpower. Exalted and Vargottama, better. Exalted, Vargottama and in Pushkaramsha, fantastic.

His own reality check, run twice

Now count how many people that should liberate

Ketu Maharaj will sit in the 12th from the Atma Karaka in one chart out of twelve, because he has to sit somewhere. Three of the twelve signs are fiery, so a fiery one is one in four of those. Multiply, and you are at one in forty-eight. "Every forty-eighth person should get liberated. It does not look like that in our class. Eight or ten people should be free by now."

So add the next condition, benefic drishti. There are three possibilities: a benefic drishti, no drishti, or a malefic drishti. That is one in three. Multiply again and you are at one in one hundred and forty-four. Still not so. Even then, two or three of us should have made it.हिHindi class

Again take the ten bodies, and see which third of the zodiac they fall into in the Navamsha chart. It tells you which shakti you actually carry, and, before any business, what kind of partner you need beside you.

Rashis 1 to 4

Brahma shakti, creation

You can create new things. Five bodies here and you are a creative engine, but sustenance is not your cup of tea.

Rashis 5 to 8

Vishnu shakti, sustenance

Holding, maintaining and managing what already exists.

Rashis 9 to 12

Rudra shakti, dissolution

Very strong in army, police and spies. Fits businesses of disposal, demolition and shredding.

His worked case: three bodies in Brahma, one in Vishnu, five or six in Rudra. That person creates about 30 percent, maintains 10 percent of it, and dissolves 60 percent. How can they build an empire, unless the nature of the business is destruction?

The practical use. Make an informed decision and partner with somebody whose forte is sustenance. He notes what usually goes wrong: creative people find good managers very irritating, because they are dynamic and think "what kind of dumb person is this, why can't he do this or that?" There is a lot of difference of opinion. But if those two can partner, they can really build a big empire.

Career is an advanced topic still to come. As with everything else here, these are add ons. When twenty or thirty factors are brought together and you are totally confused which way to go, these small factors show the inclination. Assistant knowledge, serving the main frame of knowledge.

Chapter eleven

The heart, and how to get good at this

One more house before the method, and it is the one he called the trigger point of the whole Navamsha.

4, 8 and 12 of the Navamsha are its moksha trikona, and among them the trigger point is the fourth.हिHindi class

The 4th of the Navamsha is your heart. Your antah hridaya, the inner heart, the place where God sits. Which is why a graha sitting there needs purification if you are walking a spiritual path: without purifying the heart there is no samadhi and there is no mukti. He was careful about the phrasing when asked. It is not that you must. It is that if you are spiritual, you will not be able to move forward without it.

And the 10th of the Navamsha is the other end of the same thread: what you did, and whom you served, in your past lives. We did a great many things, good ones too, and we have brought a selection of those karmas with us, which is why the chart looks the way it does. Our chart is made from our prarabdha, and everything we ever did cannot reflect in it at once.

Closing advice

He has seen students who have been learning Jyotish for ten or twelve years and still cannot read a chart properly, and the reason is always the same: they rely only on logic. There are logics, of course, obvious things you simply apply and see the result. But delving only into logic will lead you astray. See people's charts, look at their lives, and observation gives you the confidence in what you have learned. Otherwise you stay in "this can also happen, that can also happen" forever, and the confidence never comes.

"If I say something in a consultation, sometimes I am so confident that come what may, this is going to happen. Come back to me after six months and tell me if it has not happened. I am confident because I have seen it happening in so many charts."

How long does it take?

At the contact classes at the ashram there was a meeting with Gurudev. The best students, a full year of study behind them, had understood everything and could conclude quite well by then. So they asked: Gurudev, we have learned all of this, but how do these skills come? How does the intuition come?

Gurudev answered in one line: "Look, this has to be done your whole life. Ask him how many great scholars come to the ashram. Nothing happens in one year." He did not say it bluntly, but he made them realise it. Then he told him: ask them, look how many scholars of seventy and eighty come here, people who have given their whole lives to Jyotisha.

Which is what colleges used to tell us, he said. What you study in college is very basic, it is the beginning of learning. The real learning starts when you begin the experience: the job, the interaction with people. "I am preparing an opening for you, so that you can begin well. Learning goes on for a whole life."

The mark of a knowledgeable person is precisely this: after a while you begin to see how much you know and how much you do not. Talk to the truly great people in this field and they will say they only know the surface of it.

Which is also why he has little patience for what is sold outside. Learn to read your chart in three days, in one week. He registered for one of them himself, a thousand rupees, just to see. They teach you that these are the houses, and nothing more; about as much as he gives away in a free two hour workshop while travelling. It is not exactly rubbish. It is a trailer: it creates the interest, and it is a genuinely enjoyable subject, so people come out feeling they understood something. Now you know how much there actually is to understand.हिHindi class

Venus rules both my 4th and my 9th. Does that one common lord make the connection for Gajakesari?

No. One graha ruling both is not a combination. A yoga needs two energies meeting and focusing in one place. It takes two to tango. The karmic connection between the two rashis is always there, and the rashis do aspect each other, but that is a different matter, and it is not a yoga by itself.

Then he turned it around, which is the more useful half: when you are talking about your own chart, you should not be confused. You know what Gajakesari is and how it manifests. Is it manifesting in your life? Do you feel confident and calm? Gurudev's own image for it, from a recent talk: be like a lion, not a cock running here and there. Be lazy, but be confident, knowing that whatever has to come to me will come to me. If that faith and grace is there, the yoga is manifesting.
