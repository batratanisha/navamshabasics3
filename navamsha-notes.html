Chapter one

The chart you cannot touch

Navamsha is your subtle sharira, your subtle existence, your mantra sharira. It also carries very high importance whenever marriage is being analysed. Both are true at once, and which one is speaking depends entirely on the question you brought.

By now you should be able to look at the degrees of a graha and estimate for yourself which navamsha it falls in, whether it is Vargottama or not, whether it sits in Pushkaramsha. That much is assumed. What follows goes further than a first semester normally goes, and he said so himself: "I have never taught this in first semester. I know it is too little, too early, but still I wanted to share."

Because the chart is subtle, it is also delicate. Two questions from the room made that very concrete.

?

Two babies born in the same hospital, in rooms side by side. Same planets, same everything. Will they have the same life?

It cannot happen in the same moment. If at all, there has to be a difference of 24 seconds, one vighati. Only one human soul can come in each vighati. An animal soul can, but only one human soul. "A universal law which nobody knows."

And the vighati moves with space: every 27 to 28 kilometres, the local time shifts by that same 24 seconds. So two babies can be born in the same second if they are that far apart. Watch a population clock and five numbers appear at once; they are in different parts of the world.

Past that sits Nadi amsha, each navamsha broken into twelve, so one rashi in 108 pieces. Understand that sensitivity. Both are very important for tapping the supra conscious level, the depth, the karmic account. And we do nothing with them, because we are not sure of the birth time.

When a student's time had been shifted by about a minute and a half by her mentor, purely from reading her career, and it then also explained her Caesarean birth, his answer was to rely on it. As she learns more, she will see for herself that it was the right thing.

Chapter two

One house, read from three places

Last week covered the 10th, the 4th, the 7th, the 8th and the trine of 1, 5, 9. One house in Navamsha had still not been given its due, and it is the one nobody talks about.

From D1 Lagna

The gross letting go

What one gives up and renounces. Expenditure, withdrawal. Your letting-go ability in the material world.

From D9 Lagna

The subtle letting go

Sleep, bed, private life, the intimate dimension. Letting go of the subtle body, and of the subtle ego, when you have no idea of yourself at all.

From Karakamsha

The real dissolution

Moksha, emancipation, release of the soul. The true ahamkara antakara: the dissolution of the self happens here.

The double edge of the D9 twelfth

Letting go of the subtle ego is good for spiritual growth. But your ego is also what keeps you on dharma; ego gives you commitment. Losing it in the subtle can lead you astray from dharma as well. This is why combinations in the 12th house of Navamsha can cause promiscuity in marriage. Good for spirituality, not good for marriage.

And count it from the partner. The 7th of Navamsha is your partner, so your 12th is the 6th from them, the enemy of your partner.

When I teach this one, nobody talks about the 12th house of Navamsha anyway.

Chapter three

Karakamsha, where the whole chart looks

There is one point in Navamsha that is the most crucial of all. The moment it is defined, your entire focus goes there: the rashi holding your Chara Atma Karaka in D9. Karaka plus Amsha gives Karakamsha. This is your spiritual seed.

Chara Atma Karaka → its rashi in D9 → Karakamsha → the environment working on your Atma

The bhava it falls in matters. But the rashi matters more, because the rashi is the environment, and the environment constantly affects and changes whoever sits inside it. This is your subtle existence. That is where your Atma is.

Which gives the striking consequence: whoever you are in this lifetime, what you will be in the next is seen from the rashi of the Atma Karaka. If it sits in a Venus amsha, then slowly you are turning into a Venus Atma Karaka. This entire life journey, this whole life experience, is turning you into that graha's energy, because it works at a deeper level. In a Venus rashi the Atma aligns with Bhuvar Loka: material pleasure, relationship, beauty, glamour, music, art. In a Sun amsha, with the Manipura, probably Swarga Loka.

He was careful here. There are scriptural references that claim to show which loka a soul came from and which it is going to. There is no evidence; nobody comes back to confirm it. Very specific grahas are assigned to each loka in different texts, and looking at real lives he could not relate to them, so the whole system was left out of this curriculum. Someone good, kind and spiritual would show as heading to Narka Loka. We stay with the rashi placement only, because that much is logical.

A rashi he was asked about

Meena amsha

Maharishi Parashara: one whose Atma Karaka sits in Meena amsha is a very mature soul. Spiritual, detached, knowledgeable, pious. Usually a combination of moksha after this life, the pinnacle of spiritual maturity. When it is also aspected by a benefic, he says the native gets liberated.

"I don't know what moksha is, but that is what is mentioned." Take it with a pinch of salt, and say there is a good possibility of liberation.

The story he told

Snakes and ladders was a spiritual instrument first

The game we know is the frivolous version of Moksha Sopan. The original had 72 boxes, eight and nine, and each row carried the nine qualities of one chakra, from Muladhara upward, with the eighth level above everything.

You play it with something personal that has held your energy a long time, a ring, an earring, something that can represent you. After five or six rounds of dice it shows you which chakra your energy is actually stuck at. He taught it in the ashram, where it became very popular. In Nepal he built it human size on a 25 by 25 foot platform, and people stood inside the board and played, and had amazing experiences of where their lives were moving and where they were stuck. He shared it with Gurudev about fifteen years ago, and Bharatma kept the board and the book.

The lesson from the board. In the Agnya chakra there is a ladder of bhakti that leads straight to moksha, and right beside it a snake of arrogance, because bhakti brings a little egoistic arrogance too. So even if your chart shows the Atma has reached the pinnacle in Meena amsha, that is not a guarantee. It means you have reached there. Malefics and malefic aspects are the snakes that pull you back, and you begin again. Equally, if you are not there, a ladder may still carry you up: an exchange. And that exchange can be aspected by a node, a snake sitting there, biting you back down.

There is nothing certain till you are enlightened. The only certainty is the presence of a Guru who will eventually lead you. Not necessarily this lifetime.

On how to hold all of it

?

My Atma Karaka is Rahu and it is sitting in my Lagna in D9. Does that mean anything?

It means a lot. Scripturally, Atma Karaka in the Lagna or the 7th house of Navamsha shows a person who rises to royalty. With it in the Lagna you are usually not born into a mediocre family at all; you are born with a silver spoon. In the 7th you may be born mediocre and still rise to royalty.

Then come the buts. Is that graha clearly the Atma Karaka, or could a few minutes of birth time change the navamsha degrees and swap it? And it has to be supported: the dhana yogas, the Lagna rashi, the Atma Karaka's overall strength and status, and then Rahu Maharaj arriving here on top of all that. The Atma Karaka has to go into some bhava, after all. Every sixth person does not become a king.

Chapter four

What can actually dissolve it

The Atma Karaka represents your agyan, your ignorance. The rashi it sits in changes that ignorance but never dissolves it. The only thing with the ability to dissolve it is the 12th from Karakamsha. Its graha, its dignity, its natural status tell you whether the ignorance goes at all.

He worked the elements out loud. Can anything be completely dissolved in water? Water takes what is saline or sugary, and a great deal of karma is like that. Plastic will not dissolve. Wood and metals take a very long time. Air cannot dissolve at all; it depletes earth, slowly. What dissolves immediately is fire. That is why fire is called Pavaka, the purifier: the one that purifies by dissolving the impurities. That is also why tapah is done by sustaining and igniting five kinds of fire within us.

So a fiery graha in the 12th from Karakamsha gives a good possibility of dissolution. In a watery sign the fire is not lit properly. In an airy sign there is too much air and the fire is unsteady. A fiery graha in a fiery sign works fantastic.

Water Jala

Dissolves what is saline or sugary, and a lot of karma is of that kind. Plastic, wood and metal simply sit in it.

Air Vayu

Cannot dissolve. It depletes earth, slowly and gradually, and takes a long time doing it.

Sun's fire Jatharagni

The digestive fire, which is fasting. It takes physical toxins and what sits on the borderline of subtle and physical. Not all karma: the Buddha fasted for many years, and was enlightened only after he ate, when a village woman offered him a little rice and salt. Documented in the Buddhist texts, not hearsay.

Mars' fire Ambition

Destroys lethargy for certain, and a great deal besides. But while the fire of ambition is lit, how can desire be let go? Even when you resist it, much remains. Not the final thing.

Ketu's fire Moksha Karaka

The last fire, the one that dissolves everything. It turns everything into smoke and ash, and it does not dissolve selectively. Anything and everything that goes into it is gone, in the grosser world and in the subtler world both. This is why Ketu Maharaj is the significator of enlightenment.

When Ketu transits your Moon you feel everything shattering, your world shattered. Unless you have already given up this world from your heart. Say "this is not my world" and Ketu Maharaj will not touch it. Say "mine, mine, mine" and everything gets burned.

An exalted graha in the 12th from Karakamsha shows the person going into that kind of realm at a higher level. Conjunction or exchange with Ketu and you turn into a Devanshu. Atma Karaka exalted shows you are not an ordinary soul: great sankalpa shakti, great willpower. Exalted and Vargottama, better. Exalted, Vargottama and in Pushkaramsha, fantastic.

One in forty-eight should be enlightened. They are not.

Parashara says Ketu Maharaj in a fiery sign in the 12th from Atma Karaka. If that alone were enough, every twelfth person would be enlightened. He has seen plenty of people with Ketu sitting there, his own family and extended family, people he knows personally, with nothing spiritual about their lives. Mildly religious at most.

The method, not the rejection. We cannot deny the Rishis' words. Maharishi Parashara has said this, we follow it and we honour it, and we also look for the other clues. Assess every rule practically. Do not apply a sutra in isolation. Next semester is exactly about looking at the whole thing together: all the graha placements, your spiritual bent, your spiritual intensity, and only then this.

If there is no graha in the 12th from Karakamsha, look at the drishti falling on it. If that is thin too, the analysis widens: the 5th house, the 9th house, and overall the 10th and the 4th of Navamsha, all taken together.

?

My Atma Karaka is Vargottama, so the 12th from it is the same rashi in D1 and D9. But there is no graha there at all.

If the Atma Karaka is Vargottama then that is simply how it will be, and a Vargottama Atma Karaka is a good thing in itself. Empty of grahas is not empty: there is still a rashi, and there are drishtis falling on it. Use rashi drishti as well. That is how you go into deeper analysis.

Chapter five

Who is holding your hand

That whole analysis of the 12th from Karakamsha is for one thing: knowing which divine energy will lead you towards emancipation. Ishta means favourite. The one who is your favourite, or the one whose favourite you are. Your guiding angel, protecting you materially and spiritually, all your life.

How to weigh it. Highest importance to the graha sitting in that house. If several sit there, the strongest of them. If one sits there and others aspect, take the drishti in as well. Then the rashi: a male or a female rashi points to the same divinity in masculine or feminine form. A lot goes into it, and it is all calculation and analysis. Chapter six

Five ways a graha's dignity moves

Straight from the slide. Do not overstress the terminology. Just know that each one is adding beneficence or maleficence to a graha's dignity, and that is all.

Shubhamsaand Papamsa
In the rashi of a natural benefic it is Shubhamsa, and the more grahas there the better. In the rashi of a natural malefic it is Papamsa.
Bhavatmaka Navamshathe bhava it lands in
More grahas in Kendramsa, Konamsa, Labhamsa, Bhagyamsa and Lagnamsa gives better results. Worked out below on the class's own charts.
Ucchamsaexaltation
The graha's dignity in navamsha. If it goes into its exaltation rashi in D9 that is Ucchamsa, and it improves the graha's dignity.
Same tattvatrines
In trines from its D1 position, so the same element. Motivation is charged. He calls this a second grade Vargottama.
Same modekendras
In kendra from its D1 position, so the same modality. It fuels the graha's creation (cardinal), sustenance (fixed) or transformation (dual) ability.

Bhavatmaka Navamsha, the one move to learn

This is not a house to house comparison. He said it plainly when someone tried to count 5 to 9 as a trine: "I am not comparing from here to here." Take the graha's rashi in the Navamsha chart, find that same rashi in your D1 chart, and see which bhava it occupies. That bhava names the amsha.

graha's rashi in D9 → find that rashi in D1 → which bhava is it? → that is the amsha

The rule that comes out of it: if the graha lands in a Kendra, a Kona, the 11th, the 9th or the Lagna, better results, and the dignity goes up. If it lands in the 6th, 8th or 12th, the dignity goes down.

?

One more interesting thing about Navamsha, which he stopped to add himself: I don't know why I forgot this.

If a graha sits in a rashi in your Lagna kundali, and in that same rashi in Navamsha another graha is sitting, the second one is fuelling the first from the subtle.

Jupiter in Simha in D1, Venus in Simha in D9. Jupiter's expression is teaching, and when you teach, your creativity feeds the way you present: the aesthetics of it, the design of it. That subtle support is coming from Venus. The top layer and the bottom layer, one holding the other up.

Chapter seven

The 64th Navamsha, or Kharamsa

Everyone flees from this one. The whole circle holds 108 padas. Count 64 from your Lagna pada: seven rashis take you to 63, so the 64th always lands on the same pada number of your 8th rashi. Khara means mischievous.

Worked in class

Makara Lagna, 17°

17° falls in the 6th pada of the rashi, which runs 16°40′ to 20°. Her 8th house holds Leo. So the 6th pada of Leo, 16°40′ to 20° of Leo, is her Kharamsa. A papa graha transiting there brings something very difficult.

Worked in class

Pisces Lagna, 24°08′

That is the 8th pada. Her 8th house holds Libra. So the 8th pada of Libra, 23°20′ to 26°40′, is the Kharamsa. Even when the transit table says the star is fine and the chakra is fine, this particular pada will be difficult.

The shortcut, when a graha is sitting there

Nakshatras can make the pada counting confusing, so use this instead. A graha in the 8th house of D1 that also goes into the 4th house of Navamsha is a Khara graha. "Figure out the maths. I am not explaining it." The graha itself is Khara, not an easy graha: the karakatvas around it become permanently vulnerable, and it can cause a lot of challenges in life.

Rashi chart, D1

and also

Navamsha, D9

Khara grahathe 8th house of the rashi chart, and the 4th house of Navamsha. Constantly causing vulnerability.

Khara grahathe graha itself
Constantly causing vulnerability. The karakatvas around it stay exposed, and this graha can actually cause the trouble.
Khareshathe lord
The rashi lord of the Navamsha 4th house, because the 64th navamsha usually falls there. It adds maleficence to that graha's existing dignity by rulership alone, exactly like owning a 6th, 8th or 12th. Not every graha sitting in the D9 4th is a Khara graha, but the 4th lord is always Kharesha. You already know how to purify this.
Khara transitthe trigger
A malefic passing through the 64th pada, Rahu, Ketu, Shani or Mangal especially, can trigger events that really make life miserable. Passing over a Khara graha already sitting there, it escalates the problem. Always check for this when you read transits.
22nd Drekkanathe wider ground
A rashi split in three gives Drekkanas of 10° each: 0 to 10, 10 to 20, 20 to 30. The 22nd counted from the Lagna's own Drekkana is the broader Khara territory, and the Kharamsa is a single pada inside it. Drekkana is not pada: nine amshas to a rashi, three drekkanas to a rashi.
?

Should the 64th Navamsha be checked from the Moon and from the other grahas as well?

Yes. From the Lagna pada, from the Moon's pada, and one step further, from every graha. It should be seen: whichever graha you are looking at, that graha's significations get impacted by that gochara.

His worked example: if Mercury sits in the 64th Navamsha from Jupiter, Mercury acts as Khara to Jupiter. You want to study Jyotisha, and your friends and cousins, who are also Mercury, say "what are you doing, these are stupid things, leave it and let's go party." Mercury is still what gives you grasping power, so your intelligence is not against you. It works against you through its other karakatvas. That is how you learn which signification belongs in which context.

But do not take on too much right now. Practise it with the Sun and the Moon, because you experience life through your mind and your intellect, which is why Lagna and Moon lead in gochara. After a year or two of confident practice, go deeper. "I am teaching you how to crawl, and you are asking me whether you will also be able to ski when you climb the Himalayas."

Chapter eight

Nidhanamsa and Ashtamamsa

Some schools treat these as the same thing, others separate them, and that is exactly where the confusion comes from. This is the split we follow. He settled it twice in one session, correcting himself mid sentence for saying "10th" when he meant "8th".

Counted from the chart

Nidhanamsa

the 8th house rashi of your D1

Look at which rashi sits in the 8th house of your rashi chart. Any graha that goes into that rashi in Navamsha has gone into Nidhanamsa.

Taurus lagna, so the 8th rashi is Sagittarius: any graha in Sagittarius in D9 is in Nidhanamsa. Karka lagna, so the 8th rashi is Kumbha: the Moon, which is the lagnesha, landing in Kumbha in D9 is the lagnesha in Nidhanamsa.

Read it from the Lagna, from the Moon, and one step further, from each graha.

Counted from itself

Ashtamamsa

the 8th rashi counted from the graha itself

Wherever the graha sits in the rashi chart, if in Navamsha it goes into the 8th rashi from itself, that is Ashtamamsa.

Sun in Gemini going to Capricorn navamsha. Sun in Sagittarius going to Cancer navamsha. Jupiter in Sagittarius going to Cancer navamsha.

There are many ways people count Ashtamamsa, and many opinions on it. This is the one the text clearly indicates, so this is the one we follow.

What Nidhanamsa actually does

A graha sitting in your Bhagya supports your Bhagya. A graha sitting in your Karma supports your Karma. A graha in Nidhanamsa is empowering your 8th house, according to its own attributes. That brings more karmic retribution to you, more suffering, more misery. Several grahas there simply means more of the 8th being strengthened.

But the same strength has another door. If you are spiritual, into occult, learning Jyotisha and the esoteric sciences, this can strengthen those studies. Nidhanamsa can lead you there too. Retrograde in Nidhanamsa means you make more effort, repeated effort, to come out of the misery or to learn. And a yogakaraka going into Nidhanamsa simply loses some of the beneficence it holds. That is all.

Now read the shlokas the way he does

He put these on the table deliberately, so you can see where the schools of thought parted, and so that when somebody quotes a text at you, you know how to counter it.

लग्नेशे निधनांशस्थे मूढे षष्ठगतेऽथवा ।
क्षुद्वाधया च मरणं बन्धुहीने महीतले ॥

lagneśe nidhanāṁśasthe mūḍhe ṣaṣṭhagate'thavā |
kṣudbādhayā ca maraṇaṁ bandhu-hīne mahītale ||

"If the lord of the Ascendant is situated in Nidhanamsa, is afflicted or combust, or is situated in the sixth house, there is death through hunger, or death upon the earth without relatives."

His readingThe 6th house rashi in Navamsha counts too, not only the 8th. And mūḍhe is where the schools split: some translate it as combust, hidden by the Sun's rays; others as dumbstruck, frozen, stupefied, which can come through combustion, through affliction, or through other means. He has this in his own chart. "I was petrified when I saw this first." Then he went researching and found the same in Indira Gandhi's chart, and something similar with Mahatma Gandhi. Both assassinated, both possibly not surrounded by immediate family at the end. But the situation was not what the verse literally describes. Do not take it as it is written.

पापयुक्ते तदा जातः पञ्चाशद्वर्षजीवितः ॥

lagneśe nidhanāṁśasthe lagnāṁśe nidhaneśvare |
pāpayukte tadā jātaḥ pañcāśad-varṣa-jīvitaḥ ||

"If the lord of the Ascendant is in Nidhanamsa, and the lord of the 8th house is in Lagnamsa, and the two are associated with malefics, then the native lives for fifty years."

Jaimini Maharishi's triple yoga technique is what many people use for that broad band.

He knows one or two techniques that work very well, but they have to be learned from someone who has mastered them, and the teacher they brought last year declined, saying the students were not ready. It is not in the curriculum. It may appear in the fourth semester, which stays deliberately floaty depending on student interest and on finding the right expert. They are also exploring building an AI model around it, still work in progress.

"Everything you can think of, nature has already put into Jyotisha. You can figure it out, but you have to decode it. And nature is too smart: if too many things are decoded, nature changes the pattern. The sutras that applied a hundred years ago do not work any more, because nature has changed and society has evolved."

"When Jupiter is in the 2nd house in Nidhanamsa and the Moon is in its debilitation Navamsha, the result of Eka-yoga is said to continue up to sixteen years."

Decodedjīve is Jupiter. dhane is the 2nd house. niśākare is "the one who causes the night", which is how the Moon is named poetically. ṣoḍaśa is sixteen. Eka-yoga is the yoga of poverty, so: sixteen years of it. Three words carrying three conditions at once.

युग्मे मकरांशे सूर्ये चापे कर्कटांशगे ।

"If the Sun is in Capricorn Navamsha while in Gemini, in Cancer Navamsha while in Sagittarius, in Libra Navamsha while in Pisces, or in Aries Navamsha while in Virgo, the native has short life."

Simplifiedyugma means couple, which is how Mithuna gets its other name. Every pair listed is the Sun going into the 8th from itself in Navamsha, which is Ashtamamsa. The Rishis complicated it because they were explaining Nidhanamsa and Ashtamamsa together.

"Jupiter in Sagittarius in Cancer Navamsha; debilitated in Capricorn in Leo Navamsha; or in Pisces in Libra Navamsha: causes sorrow and deprivation of learning and happiness."

"When Jupiter is in the 2nd house, having avoided Ashtamamsa, the native is happy, learned, sweet-spoken, well-mannered and devoted to dharma."

The catchDo not see Jupiter in the 2nd and say "oh, all good thing, fantastic." No, no, wait. First check that it has not gone into the 8th sign from itself in Navamsha. Jupiter in Gemini must not be in Capricorn navamsha. Only then does the good result stand.

"When Ketu is in the 8th from the Ascendant and situated in Ashtamamsa, during its Dasha and Antardasha there may be fear or experience of thieves from time to time. When Rahu is in the 2nd house, there is loss or severance of wealth during its own Dasha and Antardasha."

Why bothWhen Ketu is in the 8th, Rahu is in the 2nd, so one shloka covers the pair.

When you approach these scriptural texts you have to be a little open minded. If you stay stuck on the words as written, even with a correct translation, even knowing Sanskrit, it gets very difficult to move further in Jyotisha.

Chapter nine

What your consciousness is woven from

Open Jagannatha Hora and simply read the nakshatra pada numbers of the ten bodies, from Lagna through Ketu and no further. No Gulika, no Mandi. Each pada number names a purushartha, and the tally shows the proportion you are made in.

Pada 1

Dharma

These grahas support your dharma. Four or five here: fantastic, work on dharma.

0
Pada 2

Artha

Resource building. More grahas here, do not hesitate. Earn money, be resourceful.

0
Pada 3

Kama

Fulfilment of desire, fun, frolic, joy. You are the life of the party, and it can be done in a satvik way: organise satsang, dance along with everyone.

0
Pada 4

Moksha

Your own space, your own freedom. My time, my space. Do not read this as "very good person".

0

Tally your own ten bodies, Lagna through Ketu.0 of 10 placed

Consider your consciousness a fabric, and the purusharthas the threads that went into weaving it. Look at the ten bodies and you can see what percentage of which thread went into you. Once you know what you are made of, you know what purpose to put yourself to. A cotton cloth will be used for cleaning. A silk cloth will be used on the puja altar, in your worship place. There is no good or bad in it.

Too much moksha pada and such people are very difficult to live with. They need so much "me time" that you will never get them. He has seen people who after marriage do not want husband or wife entering their room. Then why did you get married? Zero moksha pada and there are no boundaries at all: sharing everything with everyone, not minding using somebody else's toothbrush, six or seven boys in one hostel bed lying on each other all night.

Do not correct people out of their own weave. If somebody shows a lot of kama purushartha, let them. Otherwise the life goes by and they say "I have done nothing I wanted to do", because they were living as society influenced them and not as their nature. He described parents whose son has six grahas in moksha: he loves Gurudev, Gurudev loves him, but he will not come along and will not follow the programme. These are free spirited people. You cannot bind them. They need their own world.

Chapter ten

Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra, read in Navamsha

Again take the ten bodies, and see which third of the zodiac they fall into in the Navamsha chart. It tells you which shakti you actually carry, and, before any business, what kind of partner you need beside you.

Rashis 1 to 4

Brahma shakti, creation

You can create new things. Five bodies here and you are a creative engine, but sustenance is not your cup of tea.

Rashis 5 to 8

Vishnu shakti, sustenance

Holding, maintaining and managing what already exists.

Rashis 9 to 12

Rudra shakti, dissolution

Very strong in army, police and spies. Fits businesses of disposal, demolition and shredding.

His worked case: three bodies in Brahma, one in Vishnu, five or six in Rudra. That person creates about 30 percent, maintains 10 percent of it, and dissolves 60 percent. How can they build an empire, unless the nature of the business is destruction? Some businesses are: disposal, demolition, shredding, and a lot of people in army, police and intelligence carry very strong Rudra shakti.

The practical use. Make an informed decision and partner with somebody whose forte is sustenance. He notes what usually goes wrong: creative people find good managers very irritating, because they are dynamic and think "what kind of dumb person is this, why can't he do this or that?" There is a lot of difference of opinion. But if those two can partner, they can really build a big empire.

Career is an advanced topic still to come. As with everything else here, these are add ons. When twenty or thirty factors are brought together and you are totally confused which way to go, these small factors show the inclination. Assistant knowledge, serving the main frame of knowledge.

Chapter eleven

How to actually get good at this

He closed on method rather than content, and it was the sharpest part of the class.

If you rely only on logic while learning Jyotish, you will be utterly confused. Observe more, think less.

Closing advice

He has seen students who have been learning Jyotish for ten or twelve years and still cannot read a chart properly, and the reason is always the same: they rely only on logic. There are logics, of course, obvious things you simply apply and see the result. But delving only into logic will lead you astray. See people's charts, look at their lives, and observation gives you the confidence in what you have learned. Otherwise you stay in "this can also happen, that can also happen" forever, and the confidence never comes.

"If I say something in a consultation, sometimes I am so confident that come what may, this is going to happen. You can't change this. Come back to me after six months and tell me if it has not happened. I am confident because I have seen it happening in so many charts."

You have only the first two semesters to look at charts freely on your own. From the third semester the internship begins officially, on paper, with the top tier joining live consultations, everything recorded. So these weeks are for revision and observation: no new lesson is being added this semester, the mock test link comes a week before the exam, and the question paper is being rewritten, similar in kind to what the seniors saw but not the same questions.

And the standard he set for questions from here on: do not bring him two or three conditions and ask what it means. Bring your own understanding, and he will correct it if it needs correcting. "Now you are equipped enough to start bringing things together, in whatever capacity."
