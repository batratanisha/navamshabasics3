Where we start

One house, read from three places

Navamsha is your subtle sharira — your mantra sharira, your subtle existence. It also carries very high importance whenever marriage is being analysed. And in that chart, one house had not yet been given its due: the 12th.

12 Slide

From D1 Lagna

The gross letting-go

What one gives up and renounces. Expenditure, withdrawal. Your letting-go ability in the material world.

12 Slide

From D9 Lagna

The subtle letting-go

Sleep, bed, private life, the intimate dimension. Letting go of the subtle body — and of the subtle ego, when you have no idea of yourself at all.

12 Slide

From Karakamsha

The real dissolution

Moksha, emancipation, release of the soul. The true ahamkara-antakara — the dissolution of the self happens here.

The double edge of the D9 twelfth

Letting go of the subtle ego is good for spiritual growth — but your ego is also what keeps you on dharma. Ego gives you commitment. Losing it in the subtle can also lead you astray from dharma. This is why combinations in the 12th house of Navamsha can cause promiscuity in marriage. Good for spirituality; not good for marriage.

And count from the partner: the 7th of Navamsha is your partner, so your 12th is the 6th from them — the enemy of your partner.

“When I teach this one — nobody talks about the 12th house of Navamsha anyway.”

In class

The focal point

Karakamsha — where the whole chart looks

There is one point in Navamsha which is the most crucial. The moment it is defined, your entire focus goes there: the rashi holding your Chara Atma Karaka in D9. Karaka + Amsha = Karakamsha. This is your spiritual seed.

Chara Atma Karaka → its rashi in D9 → Karakamsha → the environment working on your Atma

The bhava it falls in matters. But more important than the bhava is the rashi, because the rashi is the environment, and the environment constantly affects and changes whoever sits in it. This is your subtle existence — that is where your Atma is.

So: whoever you are in this lifetime, what you will be in the next is seen from the rashi of the Atma Karaka. If your Atma Karaka sits in a Venus amsha, then whatever it is now, slowly you are turning into a Venus Atma Karaka — this entire life's experience is turning you into that graha's energy, because it works at a deeper level. Sitting in Venus's rashi, the Atma is aligning with Bhuvar Loka: material pleasures, relationships, beauty, glamour, music, art. In Sun's amsha, aligning with the Manipura, probably Swarga Loka.

He was clear that this is scriptural territory. There are texts that claim to show which loka the soul came from and which it is going to — but there is no evidence, nobody comes back to tell us. Very specific grahas are assigned to each loka in different scriptures, and looking at people's actual lives he could not relate to them, so that whole system was left out of the curriculum. We stay with the rashi placement only, because that much is logical: the environment shapes the one sitting in it.

Meena amsha, and why it still isn't a guarantee

Maharishi Parashara: one whose Atma Karaka is in Meena amsha is a very mature soul — spiritual, detached, knowledgeable, pious. Usually a combination of moksha after this life; the pinnacle of spiritual maturity. When it is also aspected by a benefic, he says the native is liberated. Take it with a pinch of salt — say there is a good possibility of liberation.

The story he told · Moksha Sopan

Snakes and ladders was a spiritual instrument first

The game we know is the frivolous version of Moksha Sopan. The original had 72 boxes — eight and nine — and each row carried the nine qualities of one chakra, from Muladhara upward, with the eighth level above everything. You play it with something personal that has held your energy for a long time — a ring, an earring — and after five or six rounds of dice it shows you which chakra your energy is actually stuck at.

He taught it in the ashram; in Nepal he built it human-size on a 25 × 25 ft platform and people stood inside it and played. He shared it with Gurudev about fifteen years ago; Bharatma kept the board and the book.

The lesson from the board: in the Agnya chakra there is a ladder of bhakti that leads straight to moksha — and right next to it a snake of arrogance, because bhakti also brings a little egoistic arrogance. So even if your chart shows the Atma has reached the pinnacle in Meena amsha, that is not a guarantee. It means you have reached there; malefics and malefic aspects are snakes that can still pull you down and you start again. Equally, if you are not there, a ladder — an exchange — may still carry you up. And that exchange can be aspected by a node, a snake sitting there, biting you back down.

“There is nothing certain till you are enlightened. The only certainty is the presence of a Guru who will eventually lead you — not necessarily this lifetime.”

On how to hold all of it

The most important house of all

12th from Karakamsha

The Atma Karaka represents your agyan — your ignorance. The rashi it sits in changes that ignorance but never dissolves it. The only thing with the ability to dissolve it completely is the 12th from Karakamsha. Its graha, its dignity, its natural status tell you whether this ignorance gets dissolved at all.

What actually dissolves things

He worked it out through the elements, out loud. Water dissolves what is saline or sugary — a lot of karma is like that — but plastic, wood, metal will sit in it for a very long time. Air depletes earth, slowly, and takes long. Fire dissolves immediately. That is why fire is called Pavaka, the purifier: the one that purifies by dissolving impurities. That is also why tapah is done by living with, and igniting, five kinds of fire within us.

So a fiery graha in the 12th from Karakamsha gives a good possibility of dissolution. But a fiery graha in a watery sign — the fire is not lit properly. In an airy sign — too much air, the fire is unsteady. A fiery graha in a fiery sign works fantastic.

Water Dissolves what is saline or sugary. Not plastic, not wood, not metal.
Air Cannot dissolve. Depletes earth, slowly, over a long time.
Sun's fire Jatharagni, the digestive fire — fasting. Dissolves physical toxins and what sits on the border of subtle and physical. Not all karma: the Buddha fasted for years and was enlightened only after he ate — rice and salt from a village woman.
Mars' fire The fire of ambition. Destroys lethargy and a great deal else — but while ambition is lit, how can desire be let go? Not the final thing.
Ketu's fire The last fire. Turns everything into smoke and ash — not selectively; whatever enters it is gone, in the grosser world and the subtler world both. This is why Ketu Maharaj is Moksha Karaka.

Ketu transiting your Moon: you feel everything shattering, your world shattered — unless you have already given up this world from your heart. Say “this is not mine” and Ketu Maharaj will not touch it. Say “mine, mine, mine,” and everything burns.

An exalted graha in the 12th from Karakamsha shows the person going into that kind of realm at a higher level. Conjunction or exchange with Ketu — you turn into a Devanshu. And Atma Karaka exalted shows you are not an ordinary soul: great sankalpa shakti, great willpower. Exalted and Vargottama is better; exalted, Vargottama and in Pushkaramsha — fantastic.

His own reality check

One in forty-eight should be enlightened. They are not.

Parashara says: Ketu Maharaj in a fiery sign in the 12th from Atma Karaka. But if that alone were enough, every 12th person would be enlightened. He has seen plenty of people — his own extended family, people he knows personally — with Ketu there and nothing spiritual about their lives; mildly religious at most.

So run the arithmetic: Ketu in the 12th from Atma Karaka is roughly one in twelve. Three of the twelve signs are fiery, so a fiery one is about one in forty-eight. “In this class there should be ten enlightened people by now.” There aren't.

The method, not the rejection: we cannot deny the Rishis' words — we follow and honour them, and we also look for other clues. Assess every rule practically. Never apply a sutra in isolation.

If there is no graha in the 12th from Karakamsha, look at the drishti on it — and use rashi drishti as well, not only graha drishti. If that is also weak, the analysis widens: the 5th house, the 9th house, and overall the 10th and 4th of Navamsha.

What that whole analysis is for

Finding your Ishta Devata

Analysed together, the 12th from Karakamsha tells you which divine energy to align with — the one who leads you towards emancipation. Ishta means favourite: the one who is your favourite, or whose favourite you are. Your guiding angel, who takes care of you materially and spiritually, throughout life.

How to weigh it: highest importance to the graha sitting in that house. If several, the strongest of them. If one graha sits there and others aspect, take the drishti too. Then the rashi — a male or female rashi points to the same divinity in masculine or feminine form. It is all calculation and analysis; Jagannatha Hora will not deduce it for you.

Answering in the Q&A

The slide, unpacked

Grahas in Navamsha — five readings

Five ways a graha's dignity moves once you look at where it landed in the ninth division. Don't overstress the terminology; know that each one adds beneficence or maleficence to the graha.

Shubhamsa / Papamsa
In the rashi of a natural benefic → Shubhamsa; the more grahas there, the better. In the rashi of a natural malefic → Papamsa.
Bhavatmaka Navamsha
Which bhava the graha has effectively gone into. More grahas in Kendramsa, Konamsa, Labhamsa, Bhagyamsa, Lagnamsa → better results. Worked through below.
Ucchamsa
The graha's dignity in Navamsha. If it goes into its exaltation rashi in D9, that is Ucchamsa and it improves the graha's dignity.
Same tattva
In trines from its D1 position — same element. Motivation is charged. He calls this a second-grade Vargottama.
Same mode
In kendra from its D1 position — same modality. Fuels the graha's creation (cardinal), sustenance (fixed) or transformation (dual) ability.

Bhavatmaka Navamsha — the one move to learn

This is not a comparison of house-to-house. Take the graha's rashi in the Navamsha chart, then find that same rashi in your D1 chart and see which bhava it occupies. That bhava names the amsha.

graha's rashi in D9 → find that rashi in D1 → which bhava is it? → that is the amsha

The top layer and the bottom layer, one supporting the other.

Everyone flees from this one

The 64th Navamsha — Kharamsa

The whole circle has 108 padas. Count 64 padas from your Lagna pada: seven rashis take you to 63, so the 64th always lands on the same pada of your 8th rashi. Khara means mischievous.

Worked in class

Makara Lagna, 17°

17° falls in the 6th pada of the rashi (16°40′–20°). The 8th house holds Leo. So the 6th pada of Leo — 16°40′ to 20° of Leo — is her Kharamsa. A papa graha transiting there brings something very difficult.

Worked in class

Pisces Lagna, 24°08′

That is the 8th pada. The 8th house holds Libra. So the 8th pada of Libra — 23°20′ to 26°40′ — is the Kharamsa. Even when the transit table says the star is fine and the chakra is fine, this particular pada will be difficult.

The shortcut, when a graha is there

Nakshatras can make the pada counting confusing, so use this instead: a graha in the 8th house of D1 which also goes to the 4th house of Navamsha is a Khara graha. Work out the maths yourself — he wasn't explaining it. The graha itself is Khara, not an easy graha: the karakatvas around it become permanently vulnerable, and it can cause real challenges.

Khara grahathe graha itself
Constantly causing vulnerability. In the 8H of the rashi chart and the 4H of Navamsha.
Khareshathe lord
The rashi lord of the Navamsha 4th house. It adds maleficence to that graha's existing dignity — by rulership alone, just like owning a 6th, 8th or 12th. Not every graha sitting in the D9 4th is a Khara graha; but the 4th lord is always Kharesha.
Khara transitthe trigger
A malefic — Rahu, Ketu, Shani, Mangal — transiting the 64th pada triggers events that can make life miserable. If it passes over a Khara graha sitting there, it escalates the problem. Always check for this when reading transits.
22nd Drekkanathe wider ground
A rashi split into three gives Drekkanas of 10° each (0–10, 10–20, 20–30). The 22nd counted from the Lagna's own Drekkana is the broader Khara territory — the Kharamsa is one single pada inside it. Like Pushkaramsha and Pushkar Bhaga: one wide, one exact.

And not only from the Lagna. Check the 64th pada from the Moon's pada, and then from every other graha. His example: if Mercury sits in the 64th Navamsha from Jupiter, Mercury acts as Khara to Jupiter. You want to study Jyotisha; your friends and cousins — Mercury's other significations — say “these are stupid things, leave it, let's go party.” Mercury is still needed for grasping power, so the intelligence is not against you; it works against you through its other karakatvas. That is how you learn which signification to read in which context.

Mid-lesson

Two names people confuse

Nidhanamsa & Ashtamamsa

Some schools treat these as the same thing; others separate them, which is exactly where the confusion comes from. This is the split we follow — he settled it twice in the Q&A.

Counted from the chart

Nidhanamsa

Look at which rashi sits in the 8th house of your D1. Any graha that goes into that rashi in Navamsha has gone into Nidhanamsa.

Taurus lagna → 8th rashi is Sagittarius → any graha in Sagittarius in D9 is in Nidhanamsa. Karka lagna → 8th rashi is Kumbha → the Moon (lagnesha) landing in Kumbha in D9 is the lagnesha in Nidhanamsa.

Can also be counted from the Moon, and one step further, from each graha.

Counted from itself

Ashtamamsa

Wherever the graha sits in the rashi chart, if in Navamsha it goes to the 8th rashi from itself, that is Ashtamamsa.

Sun in Gemini going to Capricorn navamsha. Sun in Sagittarius going to Cancer navamsha. Jupiter in Sagittarius going to Cancer navamsha.

There are many ways people count Ashtamamsa; this is the one the text clearly indicates, so this is the one we follow.

What Nidhanamsa actually does

A graha sitting in your Bhagya supports your Bhagya; a graha in Karma supports your Karma. A graha in Nidhanamsa is empowering your 8th house — according to its own attributes. That brings more karmic retribution, more suffering, more misery. Multiple grahas there simply means more of the 8th house being strengthened.

But the same strength has another door: if you are spiritual, into occult, learning Jyotisha and the esoteric sciences, this can strengthen those studies. Nidhanamsa can also lead you there. Retrograde in Nidhanamsa → you make more effort, repeated effort, to come out of the misery or to learn. A yogakaraka going into Nidhanamsa simply loses some of the beneficence it holds — that's all.

क्षुद्वाधया च मरणं बन्धुहीने महीतले ॥

lagneśe nidhanāṁśasthe mūḍhe ṣaṣṭhagate'thavā |
kṣudbādhayā ca maraṇaṁ bandhu-hīne mahītale ||

“If the lord of the Ascendant is situated in Nidhanamsa, is afflicted / combust, or is situated in the sixth house, there is death through hunger, or death upon the earth without relatives.”

His readingThe 6th house rashi in Navamsha counts too, not only the 8th. And mūḍhe is where the schools split: some translate it as combust, hidden by the Sun's rays; others as dumbstruck, frozen, stupefied — which can come through combustion, or through affliction, or other means. “I was petrified when I saw this in my own chart.” Then he researched: Indira Gandhi had it, Mahatma Gandhi something similar — both assassinated, both possibly not surrounded by immediate family at the end. But the situation was not what the verse literally describes. Do not take it as written.

His readingSuch a bold statement to make. Many texts talk about death like this — do not trust anyone. He has tested many of these theories and algorithms and most of them do not work. Know these as contributors to longevity; don't take them at face value.

The Rishis complicated it because they were teaching Nidhanamsa and Ashtamamsa together.

The method for reading shlokas

A small technique with a large use

What your consciousness is woven from

Open Jagannatha Hora and simply read the nakshatra pada numbers of the ten bodies — from Lagna through Ketu, and no further; no Gulika, no Mandi. Each pada number names a purushartha, and the tally shows the proportion in which you are made.

Dharma

These grahas support your dharma. Four or five here — fantastic, work on dharma.

Artha

Resource building. More grahas here — do not hesitate. Earn money, be resourceful.

0

Kama

Fulfilment of desire, fun, frolic, joy. Not bad at all — you are the life of the party. It can be done in a satvik way: organise satsang, dance along with everyone.

Too much moksha pada and such people are very difficult to live with: they need so much “me time” you will never get them. He has seen people who, after marriage, don't want husband or wife entering their room — then why did you get married? Zero moksha pada and there are no boundaries at all: sharing everything with everyone, not minding using someone else's toothbrush, six or seven boys in one bed in a hostel lying on each other all night.

Consider consciousness a fabric, and the purusharthas the threads woven into it. Look at the ten bodies and you see what percentage of which thread went into you. Once you know what you are made of, you know what to put yourself to. A cotton cloth will be used for cleaning; a silk cloth will be used on the puja altar. There is no good or bad in it.

And don't correct people out of their own weave. If someone shows a lot of kama purushartha, let them — otherwise their life passes and they say “I did nothing I wanted to do,” because they lived as society influenced them and not as their nature. He described parents whose son has six grahas in moksha: he loves Gurudev, Gurudev loves him, but he will not come along, will not follow the programme. These are free-spirited people. You cannot bind them; they need their own world.

For guiding someone on work

Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra — read in Navamsha

Again take the ten bodies, and see which third of the zodiac they fall into in the Navamsha chart. It tells you which shakti you actually carry — and, before a business, what kind of partner you need.

Rashis 1 – 4

Brahma shakti · creation

You can create new things. Five bodies here and you are a creative engine — but sustenance is not your cup of tea.

Rashis 5 – 8

Vishnu shakti · sustenance

Holding, maintaining, managing what exists.

Rashis 9 – 12

Rudra shakti · dissolution

Very strong in army, police, spies. Fits businesses of disposal, demolition, shredding.

His worked case: three bodies in Brahma, one in Vishnu, five or six in Rudra. That person creates about 30%, maintains 10%, and dissolves 60% of it. How can they build an empire — unless the nature of the business is destruction?

The practical use: make an informed decision and partner with someone whose forte is sustenance. He notes that creators usually find good managers irritating — “what kind of dumb person is this, why can't he do this?” — and there is a lot of difference of opinion. But if those two can partner, they can really build a big empire.

Career is an advanced topic to come. As with everything else here: these are add-ons. When twenty or thirty factors are brought together and you are totally confused which way to go, these small factors show the inclination — assistant knowledge serving the main frame.

Answered in the room

From the Q&A

Several of these are things that only came out because someone asked.

Two babies born in the same hospital, rooms side by side — same life?

It cannot happen in the same moment. If at all, there has to be a difference of 24 seconds — one vighati. Only one human soul can come in each vighati. An animal soul can, but only one human soul per vighati. “A universal law which nobody knows.”

And the vighati changes with space: every 27–28 kilometres the local time shifts by that 24 seconds. So two babies can be born in the same second if they are that far apart. Watch a population clock — five numbers pop up at once, and they are in different parts of the world.

Does Arudha Lagna exist in Navamsha too?

Yes. Arudha Lagna in the rashi chart is what people think of you — people are in the grosser world. In Navamsha, the energies are in the subtle world: it is what the Devatas think of you.

He added immediately that Arudha Lagna is a very deep concept and this was only a name being popped — nothing more has been taught yet.

Does drishti apply in the divisional charts?

Yes — drishti applies in D9 as well. And where there is no graha to read, use rashi drishti too. That is how you go into deeper analysis.

Atma Karaka in the Navamsha Lagna — what does that mean?

Scripturally: Atma Karaka in the Lagna or the 7th house of Navamsha shows a person who rises to royalty. With it in the Lagna you are usually not born into a mediocre family at all — born with a silver spoon. In the 7th you may be born mediocre and rise to royalty.

The buts: is that graha clearly the Atma Karaka, or could a few minutes of birth-time difference change the Navamsha degrees and swap it? And it must be supported — dhana yogas, the Lagna rashi, the Atma Karaka's overall strength and status — because the Atma Karaka has to go into some bhava, and every sixth person does not become a king.

My Atma Karaka is Vargottama, so the 12th from it is the same rashi in D1 and D9 — and empty. Anything?

If the Atma Karaka is Vargottama that is simply how it will be, and Vargottama Atma Karaka is a good thing in itself. Empty of grahas is not empty: there is still a rashi, and there are drishtis falling on it. Use rashi drishti as well.

Ketu is in the 12th from my Atma Karaka in a fiery sign — but it's in the 8th house of Navamsha. Isn't the 8th bad?

Ketu Maharaj in the 8th house is good. Good for spirituality — and right now the context is spirituality, because you are asking about the 12th from Atma Karaka.

“When I say good or bad, you cannot apply it universally. The moment you say it is good for marriage, I'll say no, it is not.”

Should the 64th Navamsha also be checked from Jupiter, Saturn and the rest?

It should be seen — whichever graha you look at, its significations get impacted by that gochara. But for now stay with the 64th Navamsha from the Sun and the Moon, because you experience things through your mind and your intellect, which is why Lagna and Moon lead in gochara.

“I'm teaching you how to crawl and you're asking me whether you'll also be able to ski when you climb the Himalayas.” After a year or two of confident gochara practice, go deeper.

Which Jagannatha Hora settings should we use?

Drik Siddhanta, which is the default — we are not dabbling in Surya Siddhanta for now. Beyond that: don't change any settings. There are thousands of them, and if you move things around he won't be able to help you find what you changed. Set to default and make only the changes he has shared.

Venus rules both my 4th and 9th — does that make the Gajakesari support condition?

No. One graha ruling both is not a combination. A yoga needs two energies meeting and focusing at one place — it takes two to tango. The karmic connection between the two rashis is always there, and rashis aspect each other, but that is a different matter; that alone is not a yoga.

And on reading your own chart: you know what Gajakesari does — is it manifesting in your life? Gurudev's own image for it, from a recent talk: be like a lion, not a cock running here and there. Lazy, but confident — knowing whatever has to come to me will come. If that faith and grace is there, the yoga is manifesting.

How exact does the birth time need to be for all this?

Navamsha still gives you roughly ±14 minutes to play with. The order of importance is rashi chart, Navamsha, then Shashtyamsha — one rashi in sixty. Beyond that, Nadi amsha: each navamsha broken into twelve, so one rashi in 108 pieces. Understand that sensitivity. Both are very important for tapping the supra-conscious level, the depth, the karmic account — and we do nothing with them, because we are not sure of the birth time.

On rectification: none of the techniques are 100% foolproof, and anyone claiming otherwise, he's not sure about. When a student's time had been shifted by about a minute and a half by her mentor purely from reading her career, and it then explained her Caesarean birth — he said rely on it, and as you learn more you will see for yourself that it was right.

Is the 12th from Karakamsha telling me whether I come back for the same ignorance?

There are factors first: you need a spiritual bent, the fire to actually move on this path. If those satisfy, this will definitely trigger it.

And you do not have only one ignorance. In this lifetime you have chosen to overcome this particular one. His key: any graha not forming a Mahapurusha yoga in your chart is a graha whose ignorance you are yet to overcome. If everything is exalted — Sri Ram's chart, Sri Krishna's, Gurudev's — things are different; you have already been there, and just one or two things remain.

Can a chart show the timing of death?

Very complicated, and most published techniques do not work. Even ascertaining short, medium or long longevity — with algorithms alone it fails. Jaimini Maharishi's triple-yoga technique is what many use for the broad band. He knows one or two techniques that work very well, but they need to be learned from someone who has mastered them; the teacher they brought last year declined, saying the students weren't ready. It is not in the curriculum, and might appear in the fourth semester if the right teacher and enough research come together. They are also exploring building an AI model around it — still work in progress.

“Everything you can think of, nature has already put into Jyotisha. You can figure it out, but you have to decode it — and nature is too smart: if too many things are decoded, nature changes the pattern. Sutras that applied a hundred years ago do not work any more, because nature has changed and society has evolved.”

Are the ten Mahavidyas connected to the grahas?

Yes — that was the whole reason Mahavidya was introduced in that lesson: for each graha's purification, the highest form of Mother Divine is the Mahavidyas.

“If you rely only on logic while learning Jyotish you will be utterly confused. Observe more, think less. I've seen students of ten, twelve years who still cannot read a chart properly — the reason is they rely only on logic.”
