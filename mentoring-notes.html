Chapter one

How the exam actually runs

Before any technique, the shape of the paper, because it decides how you revise.

The clock

Keep an hour for the chart

Roughly eighty multiple choice questions, then the chart analysis. Give the analysis a full hour, and do not lose that hour. If you have studied well the multiple choice goes quickly, which buys you even more time for the chart.

The rules

Closed book, no cheat sheets

It has never been the case in any past exam that you may take any sheet in. It is closed book, strictly, with cameras on you. If anyone tells you otherwise, that is inaccurate.

Before the day

A mock exam, one week out

There is a mock exam a week before the real one, and guidance on what is involved comes with it. So you will know in good time.

The reasoning behind the closed book matters more than the rule. The exam is about knowing things. When you go into the next semester you do not want to be relying on cheat sheets; you want to know certain basics. And they cannot examine everything, so they will ask about the fundamentals.

They are only going to test you on what you know. So apply what you know, and show that you understand.

On what the paper is for

Chapter two

The list you should know cold

These are the no-brainers. Some questions are so easy that simply knowing this list hands you a pile of marks, and the marks give you confidence for the rest.

And two things about degrees

One thing has been emphasised this time that was not emphasised before: being able to look at a degree and say which pada of the rashi it falls in. If you can look at a graha and place its pada by degree, that is going to be really good.

Alongside it: work out the navamsha yourself, the easy way, counting 1, 4, 7, 10. Being able to do that by hand is a genuinely good thing to have.

Do

  • Place a graha's pada by its degree, inside the rashi
  • Work out a graha's or the Lagna's navamsha by hand, using 1, 4, 7, 10
  • Know the whole no-brainer list above

Don't lose sleep over

  • Memorising which nakshatras fall in each rashi. It would be very surprising to be asked that
  • Whether to memorise or calculate the chara karakas: do whichever you are comfortable with. Memorising them is not that difficult
Chapter three

Writing the chart analysis

The single most useful piece of advice in the session, and it goes against what some people are being told.

Do not try to write about all twelve houses. It is not possible in the time, and if you attempt it you will end up analysing at a very shallow level, which is exactly what the paper is not asking for.

Instead, pick four or five prominent houses and go into real depth. Look at where things actually are. If a house is empty and has no aspects on it, you do not need to talk about it at all.

By the time you have done five houses, you have proven that you know it, and that you can explain it.

Depth means everything at once for that house: the rashi, where the lord goes, where the karaka goes, the sukshma karakas, the aspects, and the relationship between the karakas and the bhavas, all taken together. Even going into the navamsha of it.

Then spend the rest of your breadth on yogas. Dhana yogas, raja yogas, other yogas, spiritual yogas. Pick them up and name them. That is what shows the range of your learning, rather than repeating the same reasoning house after house.

The person who scored top marks last year did not write about all twelve houses either.

You do not want to demonstrate in your exam that you know the same thing again and again and again. Try to show that you understand the concept.

On what earns the marks

Chapter four

Read in an order, not by instinct

The other repeated instruction of the session. Do not jump from house to planet to house because something occurred to you. Have a list of what you do to analyse a house or an area of life, and follow it every time.

Start at the Lagna, always

It is foundational and it says a lot. Talk about the Lagna even if there is no graha in it. Then the Lagna rashi, the Lagna lord, the Lagna karaka, the aspects onto the Lagna, and the condition of that lord and that karaka.

Take the rashi apart

Its tattva, which tells you what motivates the person. Its mode, cardinal, fixed or dual, which tells you how they move. Its ruler, which colours the whole thing. Do this before touching any graha sitting there.

What the bhava itself signifies

For the first house: the self, what you think of yourself, self-confidence, the body and health. Run the bhava ecosystem over it before you personalise anything.

Now the grahas placed there

And place them from the Chalit. The house placement of any graha is always taken from the Chalit chart, which can move a graha out of the house you thought it was in.

Aspects onto the house

Both graha drishti and rashi drishti. Name what each aspecting graha brings by its nature, and check what rashi it is aspecting into.

The lord of the house, and where it goes

The lord is what you do in that area of life, where the energy of that house is projected. Follow it to its destination and read that destination as the outlet.

The karaka of the house, and its condition

The karaka is your experience of that area. Here the dignity really does matter, because you are now looking at a placement rather than an aspect. A damaged karaka undercuts everything else you said.

Then the fine layers

Sukshma karakas, the relationship between the karakas and the bhavas, the navamsha, the SAV points, and the Arudha Lagna where it belongs. Bring them into the same picture rather than treating them as separate topics.

Finally, the yogas

Dhana, raja, spiritual and the rest. Name them and say why they form or only partly form.

Let us not jump from house to house to planet to planet. Let us follow a structure, so you know: here is my list of what I need to do to analyse a house.

On method

Chapter five

One Lagna, worked all the way through

A chart was offered for practice. Only the first house was covered, and it produced enough material to fill a page, which is the whole point of the advice about going deep on a few houses.

the Lagna and what touches it

A dual, watery Lagna ruled by Jupiterwith Jupiter, Venus and Mars all casting on it, and the co-lord in the twelfth

The rashi, before anything else

Watery. The tattva tells you what motivates the person. With a water Lagna, how they feel about themselves and their whole sense of self is driven by how they feel. Compare that with an earthy Lagna, where the sense of self is driven by something more practical, by what they own or what they achieve. It also makes them intuitive: not purely emotional, but running a logic that is feeling-based. And since the first house is the body, the rashi says something about the body's composition too.

Dual. The mode is about adaptability, not about logic. A cardinal sign is dynamic, takes the initiative, takes the lead. A fixed sign is set, determined, reliable, and sees it through to the end. A dual sign does a bit of both: some flexibility, some stability.

Ruled by Jupiter. So the person is wise, spiritual, composed, compassionate, expansive, and values wisdom. Compassion especially, given that the ruler is landing on a water sign. And being the twelfth sign, generous.

And co-ruled by Ketu. So someone who internalises, detaches and spiritualises things, and who can renunciate. Knowledge, learning and teaching matter a great deal to this person.

Then the aspects onto it

Jupiter's ninth aspect. It blesses whatever it falls on, and here it falls on its own sign. That is a very positive aspect and a very good placement, and its ninth drishti bestows wealth on the area it touches. Note the priority he set: which rashi Jupiter is aspecting matters more than where Jupiter itself sits. Even a debilitated Jupiter gives a good ninth aspect.

Venus's seventh aspect. Venus seeks enjoyment, partnership, harmony, affection, mutual exchange and refinement through the house it aspects. So the person is refined. And the aspect still works even though this Venus is in poor shape, because it is aspecting the sign of its own exaltation.

Mars's aspect. Confrontation and competition. The person can be confrontational and competitive, and that is reinforced elsewhere in the chart. What would normally counterbalance Mars is Venus, holding relationship and harmony as the priority. But when Venus as a karaka is weak, that counterweight is compromised and Mars carries more of the day: being right, and having the last word, comes ahead of the relationship. The SAV difference between the two houses concerned pointed the same way.

From the first house alone, then: spiritual, knowledgeable, blessed, generous, refined, charming, and competitive. That is already a page, before the sixth and seventh houses have been touched.

The lord, and where the energy goes

The Lagna lord is what you do, and it always protects. Here there are two lords, going to two places.

One is exalted and lands in the fourth. So the person handles themselves by going inward, and finds emotional peace at home. The happier and more emotionally content they are, and the better they feel about their home, their homeland and their relationship with their mother, the higher their self-confidence rises. The energy is genuinely turned inward.

The other lord goes to the twelfth, which reads as detachment from a foreign land, a pull back towards the homeland, or a wish to spend time in an ashram and give service. Letting go of the self, and going inward, has helped this person.

The karaka, which undercuts everything

The karaka of the Lagna is the Sun, and here it sits at under one degree of its own sign, in the first pada of the nakshatra that bridges two rashis. That is Gandanta, and it makes the experience of life difficult, with a great deal of struggle. Keep Gandanta separate in your head from everything else: it is telling you the karaka is affected, and therefore the health, the sense of self and the confidence are affected, whatever else the chart says. The karaka is your experience.

On top of that the Sun sits with Saturn, and Sun with Saturn is never good, as the story of their relationship tells you. Problems with authority and with discipline: with the boss, with the father, with being under authority and with holding it.

What answers back is the navamsha. The Sun is exalted there, and that is where the natural leadership and the readiness to take initiative come from, despite the Gandanta. This is exactly why you look at both.

?

Would the dasha matter here? Would the person be more competitive during the Mars dasha?

Yes, definitely. Running the Mars dasha in the early years, that Mars energy would be very significant indeed, and the person would be quite fiery through it.

And grahas mature. With years and with experience you learn to manage these things, especially with Jupiter's blessing on the Lagna giving the wisdom and knowledge to work on yourself. The influence is real, but whether it stays permanent is another matter.

Chapter six

The rules that came up along the way

Scattered through the reading, and worth collecting in one place.

Chalit decides placementalways
The house placement of any graha is taken from the Chalit chart. A graha twenty degrees away from the house cusp is not in the house you first thought. MKS is judged from the Chalit too. The one exception noted: the padas of a nakshatra are their own matter, not a Chalit question.
Drishtis always workwhatever the aspecting graha's state
An aspect is not diminished by the aspecting graha being debilitated, or in MKS. Even a debilitated graha gives a good aspect: by definition its seventh drishti falls on the sign of its own exaltation. What matters is the rashi it is aspecting into. If it is beneficent in that rashi, the aspect is beneficial and protective for the destination. This holds regardless of the natural benefic or malefic nature of the graha.
But placement beats aspectwhen you weigh impact
A debilitated graha sitting somewhere is far more impactful in the chart than the aspect it throws. So dignity is not important when you are reading an aspect, and dignity is very important when you are reading a placement. Both statements are true at once; know which one you are doing.
MKS is contagiousby conjunction
A graha sitting with a graha in MKS is pulled down along with it. Its drishti, however, is untouched: the aspect still lands with full effect. Only the bhava it occupies suffers.
Saving an MKSwhat was applied here
A graha in a kendra from the Lagna can save it. And a graha casting rashi drishti or graha drishti onto the MKS graha, while itself sitting in its own house, helps cancel it. A karaka that is not in a kendra does not.
Neecha bhangaand what it leaves behind
Debilitation is cancelled when the lord of the exaltation sign sits in a kendra, from the Lagna or from the Moon. But cancellation is not erasure: you still struggle with that area initially, and it is the karakatva of the graha that does the cancelling that brings you out of it.
A fully manifesting Mahapurusha yogathe extra condition
The graha must be in its own sign or exalted, and in a kendra. Then the qualification most people forget: the lord of its exaltation rashi has to be strong. If that lord is weak, or in MKS, the yoga does not fully manifest.
Gandantanot only the Moon
Any graha in Gandanta suffers, and so does the Lagna if the Lagna falls there. It is the bridging point between rashis where there is no connection of rashi and no connection of nakshatra, and it makes the experience of that karaka's area difficult and full of struggle.
Retrogradethe cost
A retrograde graha needs a great deal of effort, and repeated effort, for its significations to come through.
Kendradhipati doshaa balancing problem
When one benefic rules two kendras, getting the balance right between those two areas of life is always a challenge. Where it rules the fourth and the seventh, that is home life against spouse, or mother against spouse, or happiness against marriage. The stronger that graha, the better, and if it is also the badhaka for that Lagna, the difficulty compounds.
The seventh and ignoranceread as an assignment
The seventh house shows where you carry ignorance: an area you are here to improve on. Where the seventh lord is troubled, expect a karmic ignorance around relationship, and treat it as the work rather than the verdict.
The rest of it in a line each
Rahu moves backwards, so its degrees count in reverse when you work out the chara karakas. Vipareeta raja yogas were read here from the twelfth lord placed in the sixth, and the sixth lord placed in the sixth: the signature of a person who keeps coming back from difficulty. Gajakesari forms from Jupiter and the Moon in kendra from each other, and can be present without being complete. The fifth house is the house of mantra, and activating it can shift things markedly.
Counting from a karaka, not from the Lagna

Karakaat Bhavaat

Several of the quiz questions turned on this, so hold the count itself clearly.

The wealth pool

The fourth from the Sun. Read the pool of wealth there, and note that the fourth from the Sun is very important generally.

The flow of wealth

The tenth from the Moon. The pool is one thing, the flow into it is another.

Success at work

The tenth from Mercury, because Mercury is the karaka for career.

Gains from career

The tenth from Jupiter. Which is a different question from career success, and the two get confused easily.

And the negative counts, which is where the wording catches you. Counted from a bhava to a bhava, the negative positions are the 6th, 8th and 12th. Counted from the bhava lord to the karaka, they are the 6th, 8th, 2nd and 12th. Read the question slowly enough to know which of the two is being asked.

Chapter seven

The questions, and the working

From the quiz. The value is not the answer but the sequence of small facts each one is quietly testing.

QuestionHow long does Jupiter take to travel the zodiac, and how long do Rahu and Ketu take?

Jupiter, about 12 years · Rahu and Ketu, 18

Jupiter takes roughly a year in a sign, so twelve to thirteen years for the circle. Rahu and Ketu take a year and a half in each sign, so eighteen. Add this to the list you simply know; you will get points for it.

QuestionWhich houses does Saturn favour?

3, 6, 11

The trap is answering 3, 6, 10 and 11, on the reasoning that it must be all the upachaya houses. That set belongs to Rahu. For Saturn it is 3, 6 and 11. Remember the difference between the two.

QuestionKaraka from the Bhava Lord is negative in which of these?

6, 8, 2, 12

The other two options offered were 6, 8, 12 and 6, 8, 3, 11, and both are there to catch you. Bhava counted from bhava gives the 6th, 8th and 12th. It is the karaka counted from the bhava lord that adds the 2nd, giving 6, 8, 2, 12.

QuestionWhat is the dispositor of the lord of the wealth pool bhava?

Three steps, not one

This one is pure wording, and it is testing three separate pieces of knowledge in sequence. First, what is the wealth pool bhava? The fourth from the Sun. Count it and land on the house. Second, who lords that house? Third, and only then, in whose sign does that lord sit?

A wrinkle was accepted in the discussion: where a node is co-lord of the house in question and sits in the sign of the other lord, a second answer can be defended. Which is a useful reminder that the marks are in the working, not the guess.

QuestionUsing Karakaat Bhavaat, from which bhava do you read someone's success at work?

The 10th from Mercury

The instinct in the room was to count the tenth from Jupiter, and that was corrected live: the tenth from Jupiter gives gains from career, while actual career success is the tenth from Mercury, Mercury being the karaka for career. In the chart on screen that landed on a house that was not among the options offered.

QuestionTrue or false: Venus is forming a fully manifesting Malavya Yoga.

False

Venus qualified on the obvious count. But for the yoga to fully manifest, the lord of the graha's exaltation rashi must be strong. Venus exalts in Pisces, so the test falls on Jupiter, and that Jupiter was neither strong nor well placed, sitting in MKS. So the yoga forms but does not fully manifest.

QuestionHow many grahas can you see potentially saving Jupiter's MKS?

One

Venus, on two counts at once. Venus sits in a kendra from the Lagna, and Venus is also casting rashi drishti onto that Jupiter while being in its own house, which is itself a cancelling condition. The karaka of the third house, Mars, does not qualify, because it is not in a kendra.

QuestionOn which bhavas do the combined rashi drishtis of Saturn and Jupiter not fall?

Work forwards, then take the remainder

Do not try to answer the negative directly. Write down where each one's rashi drishti does fall, Jupiter's three and Saturn's three, list them together, and then read off the houses that are left over. That is the answer.

Two details that decide it: a graha's rashi drishti does not fall on the sign it occupies, nor on the sign adjacent to it. And the answer is wanted in bhavas, not rashis, so convert before you commit.
